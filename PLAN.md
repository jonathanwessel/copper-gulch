# Copper Gulch: plan

The design is in `GDD.md`. This file is the roadmap the hourly scheduled task works through.

## How the task works through this roadmap
1. **Pick the next box.** Take the first unticked box in the first unfinished milestone. Work boxes in order for as long as the run lasts.
2. **Release.** Run the release checklist below.
   - Green: publish, tick the boxes you finished, and add a one-line `Notes:` under each.
   - Red: fix it, or stash the broken part and publish what is green. Never push red.
3. **Targets.** The last box of each milestone is its target: one measurable line, proved by a bot or a test. The milestone is done when its target passes.
4. **Target missed.** Add the fix box it needs just above the target and keep going.
5. **Decisions.** Decide sensibly and write the decision in the box's note. Never stop to ask the owner.

## Rules from the owner
The rules for every game in this folder (free forever, no personal details, American spelling, the words players see, more than one way to win, teach as it appears, smooth and direct touch, installable, release notes, saves) are in `CLAUDE.md` in the parent folder. They all apply here. These are Copper Gulch's own rules on top, and how this repo enforces the shared ones.

**Copper Gulch**
- The look: a crisp, flat pixel UI with hard 2px borders and pixel bevels, no glossy gradients or glass. Modern motion is welcome.
- Maps (GDD.md, Town maps):
  - every lane winds through the town and doubles or triples back past the towers, with buildings along it;
  - every lane has a real entrance, and raiders stay hidden until they come out of it (`tools/audit.js` fails a lane without an entrance model);
  - every map has 3 to 5 mortar pits, too far from the lane for normal towers (`tools/audit.js`, `mapRules`).
- Auto-sell keeps a tower reserve from the start of the game: goods the next tower builds, upgrades and path choices need are never sold.
- Outposts (GDD.md, Outposts): the game starts in Dry Creek Outpost (ore and stone, Crossbow only, one loop road, a small boss at wave 10), and winning it opens Copper Gulch. Each later town opens one more outpost with two raw goods for its era. Outposts keep working in the background and feed raw goods to the towns and the market. They have an entrance but no mortar pits, and the chain rules exempt them.
- Pacing numbers (GDD.md, Pacing targets): the first tower under 1 minute, Dry Creek won in 10-15 minutes, Copper Gulch wave 10 at 7-9 minutes after it opens for the active bot, no breach in waves 1-3, the first charter offered at 35-45 minutes, and the rest of the table. `tools/balance.js` proves them.
- Range and blast read Short, Medium or Long and Small or Wide; a Mortar's range reads "Long, not up close".
- The player can always see how much of each good they have (owner, 2026-11-17): a goods strip on the play screen with a count for every good the town's towers and next upgrades use, and every cost on a card shows have / need, red when short. `tools/smoke.js` checks it.
- Time away always counts, whether the app was closed or only in the background (owner, 2026-11-17). Coming back from the background catches up the time it was hidden, like a cold start, unless the player pressed Pause. `tools/smoke.js` checks it.
- Coins are one shared purse for the whole empire (owner decision, 2026-11-17): every town, outpost, bounty and sale adds to it and every purchase spends from it; a charter resets a town but never the purse.
- Call now is never locked. The player decides when the next wave comes (owner, 2026-11-17). If calling early ever makes a strategy too strong, fix it with the bonus or the wave, never by graying the button.
- Boss waves are gates (owner, 2026-11-17). A boss wave is beaten only when its boss is killed. If the boss reaches the vault or stockpile, the town drops back like a fallen vault and the next wave number never passes an unbeaten boss. Every town and outpost.
- Live numbers never change the size or place of anything on screen (owner, 2026-11-17: the goods strip "twitches back and forth" as counts grow). Every live count has a fixed-width cell, tabular digits, and a formatter of at most 4 characters.
- Every town keeps playing when you are not looking at it, unless you hold its waves (owner, 2026-11-17). Out of play and during catch-up a town fights its waves with the background battle.
- A town that loses a boss wave or its vault while you are away holds its own waves until you go there and tap Resume waves or Call now, so it pays the coin loss at most once (owner, 2026-11-17). In the town in play a lost boss also holds the waves.
- The top bar never grows with the number of towns (owner, 2026-11-17). It shows the place's name, a Map button and one back button to the last place; all other switching goes through the empire map.
- Every object opens with one tap anywhere on screen (owner, 2026-11-17: towers and buildings were hard to tap, depending on where they were). Smoke taps every tower, building and pad at every size and camera angle.
- Only the map zooms; the page never does (owner, 2026-11-17: zooming sometimes zoomed the overlay too).
- The game fills the screen after every rotation or window resize (owner, 2026-11-18: turning the phone left the right half blank). Nothing pins the canvas to a pixel size, every view measures it each frame, and `tools/smoke.js` (rotate) flips portrait to landscape and back on the title, in a town and on the map.

**How this repo enforces the shared rules**
- Storage keys are only `cg-*`. The old game's key `copper-gulch-save-v1` may be read, never written. Saves bump `v` with a migration for any change to their shape.
- `tools/audit.js`: a pure core, no other hosts, `cg-*` keys, American spelling, no personal details, an explainer for every feature, and `textRules` (no narrator lines, no bare decimals, no fact said twice on a card).
- `tools/perf.js` (headless Edge, phone size, CPU slowed 4x): median frame under 33 ms, plus the raider smoothness check (no frame-to-frame jump over 3 times the median, never standing still on most frames). It reports draw calls and triangles. `--town empire --limit 20` is the M3 frame gate: Iron Ridge in play with three towns working behind it.
- `tools/smoke.js`: Pause and Hold waves on the play screen, the selection highlight following the card, the camera keeping the grabbed ground within 4 px of each finger, the What's new card, save slots, the explainers and offline play, at phone, landscape and desktop sizes.
- `tools/install-test.js` prints ok for every release, locally and against the live site (Chrome on Android must offer "Install app").
- Release notes: `src/data/news.js` gets an entry per release (the audit fails without one), then a GitHub release and a push notification (see the checklist).

## Release checklist (every release; stop and fix on any failure)
1. `node --test test/*.test.js` passes (Node 24 does not take a bare folder).
2. `node tools/audit.js` prints `audit ok`.
3. Run the bots that the current milestone's target names. From M1 on that is `tools/balance.js` (6 seeds by default, with the strategy and variety checks); from M2, `tools/firstplay.js`; from M4, `tools/idle.js`. Write their numbers in the notes.
4. `node tools/smoke.js` ends with phone, land and desk `ok`, and `node tools/perf.js` prints ok (median frame under 33 ms; write the numbers in the notes). From M3 on, `node tools/perf.js --town empire --limit 20` prints ok too (the M3 target: Iron Ridge in play with three towns working behind it).
5. `node tools/shots.js <scratch dir>`: look at every screenshot and fix anything ugly or broken. Then a subagent that sees only those screenshots reads every visible line and flags anything that sounds like a manual or a narrator; fix what it flags.
6. Add the release's entry at the top of `src/data/news.js` (plain words), `node tools/bump-version.js "note, no apostrophes"`, then `node tools/update-test.js` and `node tools/install-test.js` print ok.
7. Commit, push `main`, wait for the Pages build, and confirm the live `version.json` shows the new version. Then `node tools/install-test.js https://jonathanwessel.github.io/copper-gulch/` prints ok (the live manifest and icons).
8. Tell the owner (changed 2026-11-17 by the owner's main session: GitHub never emails you about releases made with your own account): `git tag -a v<version> -m "<2-5 plain lines>"` and `git push origin v<version>`. `.github/workflows/release.yml` then makes the release as github-actions, which emails the owner. Check it with `gh run list -R jonathanwessel/copper-gulch -L 3` and `gh release view v<version> -R jonathanwessel/copper-gulch`. Do not run `gh release create`. Then a push notification of one line under 200 characters.

Until a tool exists, the box that creates it is the first to use it; skip the steps for tools that don't exist yet.

## Layout
- **`src/core/`:** pure, deterministic simulation.
- **`src/data/`:** goods, buildings, towers, foes, waves, the tree, laws, perks, badges and `towns/*.js`.
- **`src/render/`:** three.js scene, boards, camera, picking, the Capital, the empire map, effects and icons.
- **`src/assets/models/`:** the code-built models.
- **`src/ui/`:** DOM interface.
- **`src/art/`:** pixel sprites.
- **`src/audio/`:** music and sound.
- **`tools/`:** dev server, headless browser driver, smoke test, screenshots, contact sheet, bots, audit, version bump, update test.
- **`test/`:** `node --test` suites that import the core directly.
- **Reference:** the old game lives in `../tycoon` (frozen). It has good parts to adapt: `src/r3d/kit.js`, `units.js`, `capital3d.js`, `icons.js`, `music.js`, the grading shader, and the economy and combat formulas.
